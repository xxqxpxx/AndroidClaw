# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the AndroidClaw community will be treated with respect and dignity, free from harassment, discrimination, or exclusion.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that creates a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and inclusive
- Welcoming differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation
- Discrimination based on identity or experience
- Offensive comments or slurs
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Any form of abuse or violence

## Enforcement

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

1. Report it privately to the maintainers
2. Do not escalate publicly or engage in conflicts
3. We will investigate and take appropriate action

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and discussions
- Pull requests and code reviews
- Community events or meetings
- Any official project channels
